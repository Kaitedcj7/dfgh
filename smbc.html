Good to know

        
Questions, answered.

      

      

        
Where are you based?
Sunny's is at Purok 5, Arnaldo Hwy, General Trias, Cavite. Call 0954 443 2362 or send a booking request, and tell us where your car is parked so we can confirm we can reach you.

        
Do you really come to my home or office?
Yes. Mobile service is what we do. Home, office and company locations are all fine, and customers have booked us for personal cars and work vans alike.

        
What if I don't have enough space at home?
Just tell us when you book. We'll suggest bringing the car to our location instead so the job still gets done properly.

        
Can I reschedule?
Yes. Customers tell us booking and rescheduling is easy. Message us and we'll find a new slot.

        
Do you do ceramic coating, PPF and tint?
Yes. Ceramic coating, paint protection film and window tint are all part of the menu, along with washes and full interior and exterior detailing.

        
Do you service motorcycles and company vehicles?
Yes to both. We detail motorcycles, and we've worked on company service vehicles such as vans at the client's own location.

        
How much does it cost?
Price depends on the vehicle size, its condition and the service you choose. Send a request and you'll get a clear quote before any work begins. Customers consistently describe our pricing as fair and reasonable.
